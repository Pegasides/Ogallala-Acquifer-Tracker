Ogallala Aquifer Water Table Tracker

The Ogallala Aquifer is one of the largest freshwater resources in the world, vital to United States agriculture. This tracker monitors regional water level declines and depletion trends.

Real-time well observations and historical depletion data are maintained by federal and state geological agencies. Use the links below to access interactive mapping tools and raw datasets.
